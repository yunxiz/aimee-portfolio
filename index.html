Deforestation and carbon sequestration at Baima Snow Mountain

First-author paper, Atlantis Press (ICHESS 2021)

I did research on Yunnan snub-nosed monkey, an endangered primate, whose habitat was losing in the Baima Snow Mountain Nature Reserve. I interviewed villagers and the reserve's guards, then mapped the reserve in QGIS and found deforested land inside the core zone, the part that's supposed to be the most protected.

  • Located three deforested patches in the core zone, 0.674 km² in total, from satellite imagery in QGIS.
  • Combined GlobBiomass forest biomass data with carbon storage estimates for cold-temperate coniferous forest to estimate what reforesting those patches could sequester: about 4,800 tons of CO₂.
  • Priced that carbon on China's new national carbon market at roughly 239,000 RMB, as a way to fund reforestation and the reserve's underpaid guards.
QGIS, GlobBiomass, satellite imagery. Read the paper
